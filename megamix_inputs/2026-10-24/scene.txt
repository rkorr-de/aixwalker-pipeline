A high-floor loft in Yanaka, Tokyo, at night. Tall black-framed windows overlook the quiet old-town rooftops, a cedar-lined temple lane and the faint glowing silhouette of a five-storey pagoda far away, with the Tokyo Skytree a soft distant spire under a pitch-black night sky. Board-formed concrete, smoked oak, dark cedar and hand-glazed ceramics, a hushed warm amber atmosphere, calm and still, made for long, focused evenings.
